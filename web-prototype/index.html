      The text they would get

      In the real app this goes out by text with a live location link, and keeps updating until someone confirms they're with you. This prototype sends nothing.

      What the watch versions add (Apple Watch, Samsung Galaxy Watch)

      • Real heart rate, heart-rate variability and motion from the watch, compared to your own baseline.
      • Checks on your wrist with a strong vibration, so you don't need your phone out.
      • Texts with live location sent from your iPhone or a cellular watch.

      Still Here is open-source safety software, not a medical device. It can't tell whether a drink was spiked. It notices when you stop responding normally. Keep using drink covers, stay with friends, and call 911 in an emergency.
